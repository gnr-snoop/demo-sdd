## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an editorial, tech-forward aesthetic that merges high-energy digital directness with the tactile warmth of printed literature. Inspired by the meeting of an energetic scarlet identity with thoughtful, bookish surfaces, the UI avoids sterile neo-corporate tropes in favor of an authoritative, human, and intellectual presence.

### Visual Character
- **Editorial Substrate:** The digital canvas behaves like heavyweight, unbleached cotton paper. Interfaces default to warm cream tones rather than harsh digital whites or cold dark modes.
- **Chromatic Punctuation:** High-voltage brand red is used intentionally as an ignition point—drawing immediate focus to primary actions, interactive focal points, and statement banners without overwhelming the calm literary base.
- **Typography as Architecture:** The system relies on a sharp dichotomy between an expressive optical variable serif for titles and a hyper-rationalist geometric sans-serif for dense data, labels, and interaction.
- **Organic Asymmetry:** Structural geometry balances soft sweeps with tight corners, punctuated by a signature asymmetric border radius that establishes an instantly recognizable silhouette across cards, action buttons, and statement panels.

## Colors

The palette is constructed through high-temperature contrast: rich, tactile cream substrates meet energetic scarlet and balanced blue-slate anchors.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas (`#faf9f5`):** The foundational page background. Replaces standard `#FFFFFF` to reduce eye strain and provide an organic substrate.
- **Surface Soft (`#f5f0e8`):** Alternating full-width section bands and secondary interaction hover foundations.
- **Surface Card (`#efe9de`):** Primary structural surface for content containers, forms, and interactive blocks.
- **Surface Card Strong (`#e8e0d2`):** Active category indicators, selected filters, and highlighted visual modules.
- **Dark Surface Ecosystem (`#181715`, `#252320`, `#1f1e1b`):** Grounded obsidian surfaces reserved for global footers, deep technical code blocks, and immersive contrast bands.

### Text & Accents
- **Headings & Body (`#212121`):** Deep warm black for sharp legibility across light surfaces.
- **Supporting Copy (`#546E7A` & `#424242`):** Cool slate gray provides a soft chromatic drop from primary headings, moderating information density.
- **Heading Gradient:** Major section headlines employ a horizontal gradient from `#212121` to `#546E7A`, emphasizing editorial craft.
- **Primary Gradient:** High-priority buttons and hero anchors utilize `linear-gradient(180deg, #D9261C 0%, #A31810 100%)`.

### Brand Mark Asset Logic
- **Light Surfaces (`#faf9f5`, `#efe9de`):** Primary multi-color brand logo in pure original form.
- **Dark & Scarlet Surfaces (`#181715`, `#D9261C`):** Monochrome white asset variation applied with full inversion (`invert(1) brightness(2)`).

## Typography

The typographic system relies on a structural division between editorial expression and interface utility.

### Expressive Display (Fraunces)
- **H1 (Hero Display):** Upright (`normal` style), light weight (`300`), rendered with optical size `opsz 144` on desktop and stepped down to `opsz 36` on mobile. Kept strictly upright for legibility across large character counts.
- **H2–H4 (Section Headings):** Styled in italic display mode across variable weights. Optical size settings match scale: `opsz 72` for H2, `opsz 36` for H3, and `opsz 18` for H4 subtitles. When used in major section headers, apply the text gradient from `#212121` to `#546E7A`.

### Interface System (Geist)
- **Body & Longform:** Set in regular weight (`400`) at 16px base size. Renders clean, neutral lines that balance the expressive display serif.
- **Navigation & Interactive Targets:** Scaled to 14px (`0.875rem`) with medium weight (`500`) to guarantee legibility and crisp bounding boxes inside buttons, tabs, and table lists.
- **Badges & Footers:** Sub-labels, categories, and footer links leverage 12px and 14px sizes with medium weights and open tracking.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around an alternating banded grid model, using generous vertical breathing room (`--spacing-section: 6rem`) between distinct narrative chapters.

### Grid & Layout Geometry
- **Container Architecture:** 12-column responsive flex grid anchored to standard maximum width tiers: 540px (`sm`), 720px (`md`), 960px (`lg`), 1140px (`xl`), and 1328px (`xxl`).
- **Gutters:** Standard 24px (`1.5rem`) horizontal gutters between grid columns.
- **Header Geometry:** Fixed desktop header height of 88px (`5.5rem`), transitioning dynamically to 72px (`4.5rem`) on mobile.

### Spatial Rhythms
- **Micro Spacing (`0.25rem` – `0.75rem`):** Badge padding, compact input gaps, and icon-to-label inline pairings.
- **Component Spacing (`1rem` – `2rem`):** Internal padding within standard cards, navigation blocks, and secondary modular blocks.
- **Macro Spacing (`3rem` – `6rem`):** Container padding for lead forms, callout wrappers, and global vertical section dividers.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system minimizes synthetic dropped shadows, instead deriving visual depth through layered surface contrasts, deliberate border hairlines, and translucent overlays.

### Elevation Tiers
- **Substrate Level (0dp):** Canvas `#faf9f5` foundation and subtle section bands `#f5f0e8`. Zero elevation, zero shadows.
- **Card Level (1dp):** Surface Card `#efe9de` resting over the lighter canvas. Rather than heavy neutral shadows, elevation is rendered using a tinted blue-gray drop shadow: `rgba(84, 110, 122, 0.4) 0 0 8px -2px`, paired with a crisp 1px pure `#ffffff` interior perimeter hairline.
- **Elevated Interactive Tier (2dp):** Floating dialogs, elevated menus, and active overlays. Enhanced using soft diffuse blue-gray shading `rgba(84, 110, 122, 0.25) 0 8px 24px -4px`.
- **Glassmorphism Backdrop (Sticky Overlay):** Scrolled navigation bar utilizes `rgba(250, 249, 245, 0.85)` coupled with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` and a bottom border of `1px solid #e6dfd8`.
- **Active Focus Glow:** Interactive form fields utilize an energetic halo rather than hard outlines: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(217, 38, 28, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system is defined by a signature asymmetrical silhouette that disrupts conventional grid rigidity while preserving balance.

### Asymmetric Signature Curvature
- **Signature Cards & Primary CTA Panels:** Defined by the distinctive corner pattern `border-radius: 2rem 2rem 0.5rem 2rem` (sweeping rounded corners on top-left, top-right, and bottom-left, with a compressed 8px bottom-right notch).
- **Inverted Signature Variant:** Used across paired layouts or contrasting modules using `border-radius: 2rem 2rem 2rem 0.5rem`.
- **Interactive Morphing:** Dedicated action elements (e.g., dynamic "Go" triggers) begin with the asymmetrical radius (`2rem 2rem 0.5rem 2rem`) and animate smoothly to a uniform `2rem` pill radius on hover.

### Component-Level Geometries
- **Standard Inputs & Standard Buttons:** Uniform `0.5rem` (`8px`) border radius for balanced touch targets.
- **Pills & Badges:** Fully rounded `9999px` geometry for metadata tags, category filters, and notification markers.
- **Large Panels & Overlays:** `1rem` (`16px`) to `2rem` (`32px`) symmetric rounding for dialog windows and dark footer blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Uses the brand gradient `linear-gradient(180deg, #D9261C 0%, #A31810 100%)` with white text, `0.5rem` radius, and horizontal padding of `1.5rem`. On hover, transitions to solid `#FF6347` or `#FF4D4D`. Transition timing is standard `0.4s ease`.
- **Signature "Go" Button:** Utilizes the asymmetric radius `2rem 2rem 0.5rem 2rem`. Animates to `2rem` symmetric radius on hover, creating a fluid mechanical response.
- **Secondary Button (Light Surface):** `#faf9f5` background with a 1px border of `#e6dfd8`. On hover, shifts to `#f5f0e8` with border `#BDBDBD`.
- **Secondary Button (Dark Surface):** Transparent background with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` and `#faf9f5` text. On hover, fills with `#252320`.

### Cards & Content Containers
- **Cream Content Card:** Surface `#efe9de`, surrounded by a 1px border in `#ffffff`, utilizing the asymmetric radius `2rem 2rem 0.5rem 2rem` and shadow `rgba(84, 110, 122, 0.4) 0 0 8px -2px`. Internal padding is `1.5rem` to `2rem`.
- **Dark Surface Card:** Surface `#181715` with secondary elevated regions in `#252320`. Text defaults to `#faf9f5` (headings) and `#a09d96` (supporting body).

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Text Inputs, Selects, Textareas:** `#faf9f5` background, `0.5rem` border radius, `1px solid #e6dfd8` border. Padding set to `0.625rem 0.875rem`. Text rendered in Geist regular 16px (`#212121`).
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to primary brand red `#D9261C` accompanied by focus ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(217, 38, 28, 0.12)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `18px × 18px` custom controls with `#faf9f5` fill and `1px solid #BDBDBD`. When checked, surfaces fill with `#D9261C` displaying an inverted white glyph.

### Badges & Category Chips
- **Category Badge:** Full pill geometry (`9999px` radius), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`, font Geist medium 12px (`label-sm`).
- **Standard State:** `#efe9de` background with `#424242` text.
- **Active / Primary State:** `#D9261C` background with `#ffffff` text.

### Navigation Header
- **Placement & Structure:** Sticky positioning at `z-index: 1020`. Top-left contains the brand mark (height `2rem` on desktop, scaled to `1.5rem` on mobile). Navigation items right-aligned using Geist medium 14px.
- **Scrolled Appearance:** Translucent `#faf9f5` substrate with `8px` backdrop blur and soft hairline border bottom `#e6dfd8`.
